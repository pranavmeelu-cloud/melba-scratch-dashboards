Commentary

A genuinely good Monday, read past the headline. Victoria Street turned over $1,765.40 against $862.64 last Monday — revenue doubled, driven by $813.80 of catering against $37.04 a week ago. To serve it the site ran five staff for 38.14h against two staff for 16.30h, and still landed at −$12.93 versus −$10.74. The result is flat while the volume doubled, which is the right outcome.

Food cost was 13.43% of revenue — Gilmours $75.17 on two David orders, plus the first day of the new Five Elements coffee window ($153.64) and the last day of the Karma Drinks fortnight ($8.23, window now closed). That is well under the blended benchmark, but as the alert above explains it is structural rather than a buying achievement: the catering food is costed in the Scratch Bakers kitchen.

Wednesday. The model would set an ordering budget of $2,495.99; the team card publishes the benchmark instead — $472.67 net of the $153.64 of coffee already committed. For Tuesday, two Gilmours orders are on the book — O-0005710269 $205.85 and O-0005710251 $340.79 — giving an expected total of $700.28 against a benchmark of $347.24.
