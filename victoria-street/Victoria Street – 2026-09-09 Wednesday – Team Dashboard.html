Commentary
Wednesday 9 September 2026

A solid Wednesday — 13.4% of revenue, the best day of the week so far, though still under the 20% target. Catering did the heavy lifting: $1,893.62 against a kiosk take of $1,113.00, so catering was 63% of the day.

Food cost was excellent at $334.88, just 11.1% of revenue. One Gilmours delivery ($181.24) and the Five Elements daily share ($153.64). No Bidfood order, and that is confirmed rather than assumed. Yesterday’s estimate for today was $351.37 and the day landed at $334.88 — within $16.49, two days running of near-exact forecasting.

Hours are what held the margin back: $1,511.70 across 54.64 hours, $425.41 above the same day last week and just over half of revenue. David Easthope worked 14.28h (06:29–22:39) on the biggest catering day of the week. Mana Cervela’s shift was never clocked out (Deputy recorded 19.97h, auto-closed at 3am) and has been booked at 8h — please remind the team to clock out, since left uncorrected it would have overstated the day badly.

Why the "below budget" figures look so large. The budget model charges Victoria Street 22% of its catering revenue as a food allowance, but that catering food is produced and costed in the Scratch Bakers kitchen. On a day that is 63% catering the allowance is large and Victoria Street has no way to spend it. It is a quirk of two sites sharing a kitchen, not under-ordering. Friday’s budget is therefore held at the plain benchmark.
