Daily. Revenue $1,954.66 – POS $1,198.80 and catering $755.86 – against $2,271.51 the same Friday last week (−$316.85). COGS $584.04, wages $946.78 over 33.07h, operating costs $495.77. Result $-71.93 (-3.7% of revenue) – the first Victoria Street deficit since Monday.

The deficit is a timing mismatch, not overspending. Catering fell to $755.86 from $2,323.55 on Thursday – a $1,567.69 drop in a single day – while buying carried on at $584.04, the second-heaviest COGS day of the week. Two Gilmours orders on the David reference totalling $450.23 landed against a day with barely any catering to consume them.

The forecasting itself was accurate for a fourth straight day. Thursday expected $562.88 of COGS today and the actual came in at $584.04 – a forecast error of +$21.16. The site is buying what it says it will buy; the mismatch is between the buying and the revenue that shows up.
