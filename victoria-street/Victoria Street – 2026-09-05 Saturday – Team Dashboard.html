Commentary

Daily. Closed, as Victoria Street is every Saturday. No revenue, no COGS, no staff rostered. Operating costs $49.36 and a result of $-49.36, which is electricity $20.00 plus the car loan $29.36 and nothing else. Margin is not meaningful on a day with no revenue and is shown as not applicable rather than as a percentage. The provisional build had this exactly right.

The week is the story, not the day. Monday to Saturday, Victoria Street has taken $13,420.51 and cleared $3,057.44 (22.8%) – comfortably the best margin of the three sites this week, carried by catering. Today changes none of that; it simply adds a fixed $49.36 of standing cost.

Nothing is on the Monday order book, and for Victoria Street nothing could have been seen. Expected Monday COGS is $8.23, consisting only of the Karma Drinks daily share. No Gilmours upcoming-orders export arrived tonight, and the Victoria Street Bidfood upcoming export covers 5–6 September, so Monday sits outside it. Read that figure as a floor.

Tuesday’s budget is shown at benchmark again, deliberately. The weekend aggregate stands at −$2,325.95, which would set an uncapped Tuesday budget of $2,549.30 – more than ten times the $223.35 benchmark. This is the same structural issue flagged for several weeks: Victoria Street’s catering food is produced in the Scratch Bakers kitchen and its cost books there, so the site can never spend its own allowance and the accumulated under-spend is an artefact. It remains open with the owner.
