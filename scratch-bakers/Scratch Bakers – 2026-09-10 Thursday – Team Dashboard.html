Thursday 10 September 2026

A hard day — −9.0% of revenue on $7,857.95, against a 15% target. Two things moved at once. Revenue dropped 16.6% from Wednesday’s $9,421.57 (catering $1,314.90 → $710.38, wholesale $3,735.35 → $2,677.87), while food cost rose from $2,740.52 to $3,423.66. That is 43.6% of revenue on food where the benchmark is 27.4%.

The extra spend was not loose ordering in the kitchen. It breaks down into three things: a Gilmours Retalix delivery of $516.29 that carries a numbered purchase order rather than a name, so it never appears in the forward order list; the first day’s share of a new MG International invoice at $133.40, which only arrived tonight; and a credit of $139.87 for Goodman Fielder and Produce Company deliveries that were expected and did not turn up.

Credit where it is due on hours. The roster did come down with the lighter day — 118.84 hours against 134.21 on Wednesday, 15.4 fewer. That is the right instinct; on a day this much softer it could come down a little further still. Friday is a genuinely bigger day: $2,262.10 of catering and $3,107.81 of wholesale are already booked.

Monday’s ordering budget is nil for Bidfood and Gilmours. Fresh produce only. The committed supplier shares plus the deliveries already expected use up the whole fresh-produce minimum. If something is genuinely short, flag it rather than ordering around it.
