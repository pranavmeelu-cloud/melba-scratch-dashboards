Commentary

Daily. Revenue $3,167.19 – wholesale $2,648.54 and catering $518.65, with the café and TVNZ closed as they are every weekend. That is +$461.88 against the same Saturday last week. COGS $226.45, wages $0.00, operating costs $1,498.20. Result $1,442.54 at 45.5% – the strongest margin of the Scratch Bakers week by a wide distance.

Two things produce that margin, and both are timing rather than performance. No staff were rostered at Scratch Bakers at all on Saturday, so there is no labour against the day. And of the $226.45 of COGS, only $23.14 is a Saturday delivery – the other $203.31 is three invoices dated 4 September that arrived in Hubdoc a day late.

The underlying pattern is real, though, and worth understanding. Saturday wholesale is produced on Friday and delivered on Saturday, while revenue books on the delivery date. Saturday therefore inherits the revenue without the labour or the ingredients, both of which sat in Friday – a day that cleared only $12.33 on $6,947.46. The two days belong together: read as a pair they show a wholesale operation that is roughly break-even, not a site that made 45.6%.

Fixed cost is the constraint on a quiet Saturday. Rent $576.34 and internal cafés $430.02 together take 31.8% of revenue and do not move with volume. That is comfortable on $3,167.19; it would turn the day negative quickly on a materially smaller one.

Week to date. $38,500.98 of revenue for $838.17 (2.2%). Tuesday’s $-1,471.65 still dominates the week.

Looking forward. Two Gilmours orders are on the book for Monday – O-0005703389 $281.23 and O-0005702215 $277.81 – giving an expected Monday total of $942.37 against a benchmark of $2,019.60. The Bidfood nil for Monday is now genuine: the upcoming export covers 6–7 September and is empty. But no Gilmours upcoming file arrived, so anything ordered today is still invisible and Monday’s ordering is mostly done on Sunday. The weekend aggregate is +$741.75 – $203.31 heavier than the provisional build showed, purely from the late invoices – so Tuesday’s ordering budget tightens to $198.79. Not at the floor, and the tripwire is at 0 of 3.
