Commentary
Wednesday 9 September 2026

Scratch Bakers turned its first positive day of the week — 5.1% of revenue on $9,421.57, the highest daily revenue of the week. Every stream contributed: cafe $3,781.30, wholesale $3,735.35, TVNZ $963.70 and catering $941.22.

Food cost was $2,740.52, 29.1% of revenue. Seven Gilmours deliveries account for $1,942.93, the rest coming from committed weekly supplier shares and one late Goodman Fielder invoice ($178.45) dated Tuesday that only reached us on Wednesday. The estimate was close — $2,672.76 predicted against $2,740.52 actual, about 2.5% out.

Wages were $3,650.14 across 134.21 hours, $350.83 above the same day last week. Revenue was strong enough to absorb it, but the extra hours are most of the gap to the 15% target.

Friday is back to the fresh-produce minimum, and Thursday’s ordering is why. Thursday’s Gilmours deliveries are already confirmed at $2,268.19 against a Thursday benchmark of $2,024.11. Because that is committed, Friday’s budget absorbs the difference: the minimum is $523.88 and committed supplier shares already exceed it, so there is no room for further Bidfood or Gilmours ordering on Friday. Buy fresh produce only, and if something is genuinely short please flag it rather than ordering around the budget.
